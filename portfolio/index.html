01 / SELECTED WORKS

Vora — AI-Powered Travel Platform

An AI-integrated travel platform designed to simplify trip planning by combining flights, train travel, and accommodations into one seamless booking experience.

Challenges

  1. Fragmented Travel Planning: Users often needed to navigate multiple platforms to compare transportation, accommodations, and travel packages.
  2. Decision Overload: Numerous travel combinations, pricing options, and personal preferences made finding suitable packages time-consuming and overwhelming.
  3. Trust in AI Recommendations: Introducing AI into the booking journey required an intuitive, transparent experience that users could confidently navigate.

Solutions

  1. Unified Booking Experience: Designed interconnected search, comparison, and booking flows for transportation and accommodations within a single platform.
  2. AI-Powered Personalization: Developed three AI-assisted experiences: an Intelligent Travel Planner, a Budget-First Explorer, and an Instant Cost Calculator.
  3. Transparent & Intuitive UX: Designed guided interactions, clear pricing summaries, and a responsive interface supported by a consistent design system.

Design Outcomes

  1. Streamlined Travel Experience: A connected journey from initial discovery to itinerary selection, booking, and order management.
  2. Personalized Decision-Making: Guided travel planning experiences designed to reduce manual comparisons and help users explore options aligned with their needs and budgets.
  3. Scalable Design Foundation: A responsive web experience with reusable components, design tokens, and documented specifications for development handoff.

Breathe — Yoga & Meditation Platform

A responsive platform designed to simplify yoga and meditation class discovery, subscriptions, and personalized learning experiences.

Challenges

  1. Complex Discovery: Organizing different types of yoga and meditation classes and courses into an intuitive browsing experience.
  2. Subscription & Course Management: Providing a clear purchasing experience and a centralized space for users to manage their classes and courses.
  3. Beginner Uncertainty: Helping users with little or no yoga experience identify suitable classes based on their needs and goals.

Solutions

  1. Simplified Browsing: Designed structured class listings, filters, and detailed course pages to support easier discovery.
  2. Seamless Management: Created subscription packages and a personalized dashboard for accessing purchased content.
  3. Guided Recommendations: Introduced a multi-step questionnaire to guide users toward classes and courses aligned with their preferences and experience.

Design Outcomes

  1. Unified Experience: A cohesive, responsive platform connecting discovery, purchasing, and course management.
  2. Personalized Journey: A guided discovery experience designed to reduce uncertainty when choosing a class or course.
  3. Accessible Across Devices: Consistent desktop and mobile interfaces, including streamlined navigation and booking actions.

CAMVVA — Fashion E-Commerce Platform

A responsive fashion e-commerce website designed to combine a seamless shopping experience with curated outfit inspiration, helping customers discover how products can be styled together.

Challenges

  1. Product Discovery: Creating an intuitive shopping experience where customers can easily browse products and explore available options.
  2. Styling Uncertainty: Helping customers visualize how individual clothing items can be combined into complete outfits using products available in the store.
  3. Seamless Shopping: Balancing a premium fashion identity with clear product information, purchasing actions, and mobile usability.

Solutions

  1. Intuitive Shopping Experience: Designed organized product listings, detailed product pages, and a straightforward shopping cart.
  2. Shop the Look: Introduced a dedicated styling section showcasing curated outfits made from matching items available in the store.
  3. Responsive Design: Created consistent desktop and mobile experiences with clear product imagery, size and color selections, and purchasing actions.

Design Outcomes

  1. Cohesive E-Commerce Experience: A complete shopping interface that combines a distinctive fashion identity with practical purchasing functionality.
  2. Outfit-Based Discovery: A visual styling experience that encourages customers to explore complementary products and discover new outfit combinations.
  3. Cross-Device Consistency: Responsive shopping journeys designed to make product exploration and purchasing accessible across devices.
